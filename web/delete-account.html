Account & Data Deletion

Last updated August 19, 2026

Open Morsebound Privacy Terms

Delete directly inside Morsebound

If you still have access to your account, this is the fastest method:

  1. Open Morsebound and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Open Account & cloud sync.
  4. Select Delete Cloud Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion.

The in-app deletion flow is designed to delete the synchronized Morsebound Firestore state associated with your account and then delete the Firebase Authentication account.

Request deletion without the app

If Morsebound is no longer installed or you cannot use the in-app deletion path, send an account deletion request to:

cselrahc.117@gmail.com

Use the subject Morsebound Account Deletion Request and identify the email address used for your Morsebound account. For account-security reasons, additional verification may be requested before deletion is completed.

Never send your password, Google password, one-time code, recovery code, or other secret credential.

What is deleted

  • The Morsebound Firebase Authentication account.
  • The synchronized Morsebound cloud learning state associated with that user, including stored mastery/retention, career/streak, and settings data that Morsebound maintains for synchronization.

What may remain on your device

Deleting a cloud account does not automatically erase local/offline progress stored on a device or browser. To remove local data too, use Morsebound’s local reset controls, clear the app/browser storage, or uninstall the app as appropriate.

Retention

Morsebound does not intentionally retain deleted cloud learning state after an account-deletion request is completed, except where a limited record may be required for security, abuse prevention, legal obligations, or provider-level backup/log retention outside Morsebound’s direct control.
