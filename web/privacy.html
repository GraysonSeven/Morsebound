Morsebound Privacy

Effective August 18, 2026

Morsebound is a sound-first Morse-code learning game by iCharles.

Local learning data

Learning mastery, reaction-time statistics, settings, streaks, and career progress are stored locally on the device or browser unless the user explicitly copies a backup.

Accounts and analytics

Morsebound does not require an account and does not include an advertising or analytics service in this release.

Android updates

On Google Play installations, Morsebound can ask Google Play whether an app update is available and can start Google's in-app update flow. Update delivery is handled by Google Play.

Audio

Morse training audio is bundled with the application. Morsebound does not record the microphone for its current learning modes.

Backups

If the user copies a Morsebound backup to the clipboard, the user controls where that backup is pasted or stored.

Changes

This notice will be updated if a future Morsebound release adds cloud accounts, analytics, advertising, or other data collection.
